Motivation. The question and the chart it produced are one object, so both live in one 1040px content column: the analysis scrolls, and the composer is docked to the bottom of that column — inside it, floating over the content, never bled across the window and never growing into the analysis. The rail is 44px of icons until asked for; the token readout is a single 11px line in the top bar.
Tradeoff. A bottom-docked composer is the shape the brief warns about, so the bounded column is doing all the work of distinguishing it: it must stay column-width and fixed-height for that to read as a tool. At 1280 the column drops to ~1000px and the header's dataset chip needs to lose its row count.
